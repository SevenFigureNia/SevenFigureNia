    🚗 AutoConnect

    Connecting people with trusted auto services fast, simple, and reliable.

    📌 Overview

    AutoConnect is a web platform designed to help users quickly find and connect with local automotive services like mechanics, car detailers, and repair shops.

    The goal is to bridge the gap between customers who need help with their vehicles and businesses that need more visibility especially those without strong online presence.

    🎯 Purpose

    Many small auto businesses don’t have websites or modern booking systems. AutoConnect solves that by:

    -Giving them a digital presence

    -Helping customers discover services 

    -Making connections faster and more efficient
